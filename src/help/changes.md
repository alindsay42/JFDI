# What's new

The newest version is first. The version you're running is at the bottom of every screen.

## 0.18.0 · 14 November 2026

- **How you've been:** a new card on the Progress tab sums up your check-ins, 48-hour answers, workouts and walks over the last 4 or 12 weeks, week by week. Any workout you felt clearly worse after is listed, and tapping it opens that day.
- **Calendar:** days you felt clearly worse after a workout are marked, and workout dots now take that day's check-in colour (Green, Amber or Red).
- **What's new:** this page. After an update, Today says what's changed.

## 0.17.0 · 14 November 2026

- **Swaps:** swap an exercise for a stand-in, for one workout, such as DB bench when the bench is taken. Close variations first, then others with the same movement, and only what your gym can do. The planned exercise's progression isn't affected.
- A stand-in starts from what you lifted on it last time.
- Your own exercises can say which exercises they're **like**, so they turn up as swaps.

## 0.16.0 · 13 November 2026

- **Weighted kit:** kettlebells, sandbags, medicine balls and anything else that comes in fixed weights, under Equipment. Exercises done with it round to the weights you have, and build reps before moving up a weight.

## 0.15.0 · 13 November 2026

- **Your own exercises:** add exercises the app doesn't have, such as an ab wheel rollout, with the kit each one needs.
- **Kit filter** when choosing exercises.
- **Plates** as something an exercise is done with, rounding to the plate sizes you have.

## 0.14.0 · 12 November 2026

- **Starting weights:** each person on a shared programme can have their own starting weights.
- Changing a programme's start date keeps them.

## 0.13.0 · 12 November 2026

- **Gyms and equipment:** your bars, plates and dumbbells, and other gyms such as a hotel's. Targets round to weights the gym can actually load.

## 0.12.0 · 11 November 2026

- **Help:** the user manual, in the app.

## 0.11.0 · 10 November 2026

- **Training blocks:** blocks of a number of training weeks ending in a deload. After a deload, the next block restarts from week 2's numbers, or a later week's.

## 0.10.0 · 10 November 2026

- **How each exercise progresses:** set the jump, the style and what happens after misses, exercise by exercise.

## 0.9.0 · 10 November 2026

- **Last time** on every set, with **Use last time** to copy it in.
- Start a **deload week** from Today when you need one.
- **Weights:** suggest the next step, or just show what you did last time.

## 0.8.0 · 9 November 2026

- **Daily check** settings: what Amber does, and the wording of each level.
- **48-hour check** after each workout.
- **Walks**, logged from Today.

## 0.7.0 · 9 November 2026

- **Progress tab:** calendar, each day's log, lift charts and goals, bodyweight and every session.

## 0.6.0 · 8 November 2026

- **Today** and **doing a workout**: targets, ticking sets, rest timer, and finishing.

## 0.1.0 to 0.5.0 · 7 November 2026

- The groundwork: people, programmes, building a programme, the training rules, backups and Rebuild Log import.
