# Help

J.F.D.I plans your training, runs each workout with you, and keeps track of how it's going. Everything stays on this device and works without a connection. Tap a heading below to jump to it.

## People

Everyone in the household has their own name and colour. The colour runs along the top of every screen, so nobody logs a workout as the wrong person.

- **Add someone:** tap your name at the top, then **Add person**. Choose a name and a colour.
- **Change person:** tap the name at the top and pick someone else. The app remembers who was last using it.
- **Remove someone:** on their **Progress** tab, at the bottom, **Remove** them. This deletes their history, so back up first.

## Building a programme

Programmes are built on the **Programme** tab. Give a new one a name and tap **Create**. You land on its **plan**, where everything about the programme is set. **Save** sits at the top of the plan whenever there are unsaved changes.

### Schedule

- **Set days:** workouts are on fixed weekdays, such as Monday and Thursday. The calendar decides which workout is due, and a missed one stays missed.
- **Ad hoc:** workouts are done in order, on whichever days suit. A missed one stays next up. **Rest days between workouts** sets the minimum gap.

### Workouts repeat every

Choose **1 week** if every week is the same. Choose 2, 3 or 4 weeks to give each week its own workouts, for example heavy deadlifts one week and Romanian deadlifts the next. After the last week, it starts again from week 1. This only decides which workouts you do, not how heavy they are; that's what training blocks are for.

Each week lists its workouts, up to 7. **+ Add day** adds one, **↑** and **↓** reorder them, and **×** removes one. Tap a workout to open it. In weeks 2 to 4, **Copy week 1 here** is a quick start.

### Choosing exercises

Inside a workout, **Choose exercises** shows two lists: every exercise on the left, the ones in this workout on the right.

- Highlight exercises on the left and press **>** to add them, or **>>** to add everything shown.
- Highlight exercises on the right and press **<** to take them out, or **<<** to clear the workout.
- Search, or pick a movement, to narrow the left-hand list.

### The workout list

Below the lists is the workout itself, in order. Drag rows to reorder them. Each row has its **weight**, **sets** and **reps** (or time, distance or calories, depending on the exercise). Reps can be a single number or a range such as 8-12.

### Supersets and giant sets

Tap **Pair exercises**, then tap the exercises to group. Two make a **superset**, three or more a **giant set**. Tap a grouped exercise again to take it out of the group. Groups run in **rounds**: the same number of sets for each exercise, one after the other, with rest after each round. Reps can differ between exercises.

### How each exercise progresses

Under each weighted exercise is a line saying what happens next time, such as "+2.5kg when you hit your reps". Tap it to change:

- **Goes up by:** add weight each time; reps first, then weight (build to the top of the rep range, then add weight and start again at the bottom); a heavy top set, then lighter sets; or keep it the same.
- **Jump:** how much it goes up, from 0.5kg to 10kg.
- **After a number of misses, drop a percentage:** what happens when it stalls. Untick it to never drop.

When you're done with a workout, tap **Done** to go back to the plan.

### Weights

- **Suggest the next step:** the app works out each target from what you lifted last time and how the exercise progresses.
- **Show what I did last time:** the target is simply last time's weight, and you decide when to go up.

Either way, you can always change the weight on the day.

### Training blocks

A **block** is a run of training weeks that ends with a lighter **deload** week to recover. Only weeks you actually train count, so a week away doesn't use one up.

- **Blocks of a number of weeks:** type how long a block is. The last week is the deload.
- **Let me start a deload week from Today:** adds a button on Today for when you've plateaued or feel worn down. The deload lasts 7 days.
- **Deload weights and sets:** how much lighter a deload is, as a percentage of normal.
- **After a deload:** either **restart at week 2** (or another week) of the last block, so each exercise starts from what you lifted that week and goes up from there; or carry on from where you were before the deload.

### Daily check

With the daily check **On**, you pick Green, Amber or Red before each workout:

- **Green:** the workout as written.
- **Amber:** a day you're less able, whatever the reason. Choose what Amber does: halve every exercise's sets, take main exercises down to fewer sets, drop the extras, or leave more reps in the tank. You also choose whether the next workout progresses as normal or repeats the weights.
- **Red:** no lifting. The workout moves to next time.

The wording of each level can be changed. When the daily check is on, each exercise (or group) is marked **Main**, **Supporting** or **Extra**, which is what Amber's choices act on.

**48-hour check:** if ticked, the day after each workout Today asks how you've felt since: Fine, A bit worn but settling, or Clearly worse. It's only kept in the log; it doesn't change any weights.

### Using a programme

**Use this programme** sets it as the current person's programme from a start date. **Change start date** moves it. Two people can use the same programme.

## Gyms and equipment

The app knows what kit you have, so it never asks for a weight you can't load. Open it from **Edit equipment** on the Programme tab, or **Edit gyms** on Today.

- **Gyms:** home is the first. Add others, such as a hotel gym, with **+ Add a gym**, starting from a copy of one you have, a typical home gym, or nothing. Home can't be removed.
- **Bars:** each bar's name, type and weight, such as a 25kg trap bar. An exercise that starts at "just the bar" starts at that bar's weight.
- **Plates:** how many of each size, counting every plate (a pair is 2). Plates from every set are used together, and only matching pairs go on a bar.
- **Dumbbells:** fixed pairs, with a tick for each pair that's there to use (untick one that's lent out), and loadable handles once you've entered a handle's weight.
- **Other kit:** racks, benches, stations and anything else, for your own reference.

Changes save as you go.

**How targets round:** a target going up rounds up to the next weight the gym can make, a drop (after a stall, or a deload) rounds down, and anything else goes to the nearest. Below the empty bar is the bar. So with no 1.25kg plates, a barbell goes up in 5kg steps.

## Today

Today shows what's next, with the targets for each exercise.

- **Training at:** with more than one gym, pick where you're training. Targets round to that gym's kit, and the app remembers your pick until you change it.

- **Block and week:** next to the programme name, such as "Block 2 · Week 3 of 6", or "Deload week".
- **How are you today?** If the daily check is on, pick Green, Amber or Red first. The workout below changes to match, and the pick is saved straight away, so Red days are recorded too.
- **Start workout** begins it.
- **Skip to next workout** (ad hoc) moves on to the next one in order.
- **Train today anyway** appears on a rest day.
- **Do a different workout today** (set days) runs another day's workout.
- **Make this a deload week** starts a 7-day deload, if the programme allows it. **End deload** finishes it early.
- **New block:** at the start of a block, a note says which week's numbers it's starting from. **Use week 3's numbers** moves it on a week at a time.
- **Walks:** log a walk with its minutes. On a planned walk day this is shown straight away.

## During a workout

- **Tick a set** when it's done. The rest timer starts, and beeps and vibrates when rest is over.
- **Change the weight, reps or RPE** on any set before or after ticking it. RPE is how hard it felt, out of 10.
- **Last time:** each set shows a faint note such as "last 45kg × 5". Tap it to copy just that set's numbers in. **Use last time** at the top of an exercise (or group) fills every set not yet ticked. If your last workout was a deload, it shows the one before and says so.
- **+ Set** adds a set, and **×** removes one. In supersets and giant sets, **+ Round** adds a round and **×** removes a single line.
- **Remove** takes out a whole exercise or group, with an optional reason. Sets already done are kept. **Undo remove** brings it back.
- **+ Add an exercise** adds one that wasn't planned.
- **Notes** are saved with the workout.
- **Finish workout** when you're done. **End early** keeps what you've done. **Discard** throws the workout away, as if it never started.

## Progress

### Calendar

Four weeks at a time, Monday first. Use ‹ and › to move back and forward, and **This week** to return. Each day shows its workouts (coloured by that day's check), check-ins, walks, and planned workouts ahead. Tap a day to open its log.

### A day's log

Shows each workout that day, the check-in, walks and notes, plus any 48-hour answer. **Edit** a finished workout to change weights, reps, RPE, sets, notes or the 48-hour answer; **Save changes** lights up once something has changed. **Delete** removes the workout. Walks can be added to or removed from any past day here.

### Lift progress

Pick an exercise to chart it over time, as **Estimated max** (your estimated one-rep max) or **Heaviest weight**. Tap points on the chart to see each session. Set a **goal weight**, with an optional date, and it's drawn as a dashed line. **Every session** lists the numbers behind the chart.

### Bodyweight

Add a weigh-in with its date, and whether it was fasted. The chart shows your weight over time. **Set a goal weight** with a date (and a name if you like). Each goal is a diamond on the chart, and a shaded cone shows a reasonable pace from where you started: +0.5 to +1kg a month when gaining, 0.5% to 1% a week when losing, or within 1kg when holding. The summary says whether your latest weigh-in is on, above or below that pace, and what's left for the next goal.

### All sessions

Every workout, newest first. Tap one to open its day.

## Backup

Everything is kept on this device only, so back up now and then. The app reminds you if it's been a while.

- **Save a backup:** one file with everyone's programmes and history, and your gyms and equipment. Keep it somewhere safe, such as cloud storage.
- **Restore a backup:** replaces everything on this device with a backup. Use it to move to a new tablet.
- **Import from Rebuild Log:** brings in history from a Rebuild Log backup for the current person. Importing the same file again doesn't double anything up.

## Updates and working offline

The app works without a connection once it's been opened. When a new version is ready, a banner offers **Update**; tap it when you're not mid-workout. The version number is at the bottom of every screen.

## Words used

- **Block:** a run of training weeks ending in a deload week. Also called a mesocycle.
- **Deload:** a lighter week to recover, at a percentage of your normal weights and sets.
- **e1RM, estimated max:** an estimate of the most you could lift once, worked out from a set of several reps.
- **Giant set:** three or more exercises done one after another, in rounds.
- **Gym:** a place you train, with its own equipment. Home is the first.
- **RPE:** rate of perceived exertion, how hard a set felt out of 10. 10 is nothing left; 8 is two reps left in the tank.
- **Round:** one set of each exercise in a superset or giant set.
- **Superset:** two exercises done one after the other, in rounds.
